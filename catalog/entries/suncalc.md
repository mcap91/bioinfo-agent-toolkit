## What it does

Browser-based sun position calculator and visualizer. Enter coordinates and date to see:
- Sun azimuth and altitude throughout the day
- Sunrise, sunset, golden hour, blue hour times
- Shadow direction and length on the map
- Moon position and phases

## Assessment

General utility — not AI/agent specific. Useful for planning outdoor deployments, field sampling, or any time-of-day-sensitive work.
