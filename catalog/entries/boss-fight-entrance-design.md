## What it does

A five-beat structure for directing a boss's entrance so it reads as a threat before it attacks, contrasted against a plain baseline (boss model appears, health bar pops up, nothing else happens). The beats: (1) lock the arena — collapse or seal the path behind the player so retreat is no longer available, raising stakes; (2) make the player wait — an empty arena, a low rumble, and dust shaking loose, building tension before anything is shown; (3) reveal slowly — pan the camera from the boss's feet up to its face instead of cutting to a full view; (4) name it — slam the boss's name on screen in large text, paired with a large health bar that fills from empty (rather than appearing already full) so the player feels how much health it represents, backed by low, heavy, slow drums; (5) sell the size — a low camera angle, ground-shaking footsteps, and a roar that triggers screen shake, a dust blast, and visible reaction from the rest of the arena. Presented as a reusable staging pattern: the same boss asset can read as unremarkable or as epic purely from how these beats are directed, independent of its actual combat design.
