## What it does

Article by a Monzo staff engineer describing the transition from solving technical problems to shaping systems. Presents four repeatable moves for staff-level engineering impact:

1. **Find the research gap**: Distinguish the problem everyone is discussing from the problem actually worth solving. Simple questions — "What are we really measuring?", "Why does the system work like this?", "What assumption have we stopped questioning?" — often reveal the gap.

2. **Have conviction about the right solution**: Resist incremental fixes when the root cause is structural. If a system repeatedly reconstructs reality after the fact, consider whether the thing you care about should be generated at source. The right solution is rarely the smallest change.

3. **Design for belief, not just correctness**: Alignment is a design problem. Invite challenge, connect perspectives across stakeholders, create shared understanding so people advocate for the direction without you in the room.

4. **Make yourself unnecessary**: The highest-leverage work creates systems and people that continue improving without you. Success is when other teams start asking the same questions on unrelated projects.

Core thesis: the transition from senior to staff isn't from technical to non-technical work — it's from solving problems to shaping systems.
