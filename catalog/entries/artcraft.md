## What it does

ArtCraft is a desktop IDE for interactive AI image and video creation, distributed as stable Windows/macOS releases (Linux via build-from-source). It composes AI-generated media in 2D and 3D: 2D/3D image compositing, image-to-3D-mesh, character posing and identity transfer, kitbashing-based scene blocking, background removal, text-to-image, prompted/canvas image editing (masks, inpainting), and image-to-video. It integrates roughly 62 third-party generative models across providers — image (Nano Banana, GPT Image, FLUX, Seedream), video (Seedance, Kling, Veo, Sora, Vidu, MiniMax, Flux 3), music/sound (Suno, Seed Audio), 3D mesh (Hunyuan 3D, Tripo3D, Meshy, Rodin), and world/Gaussian-splat generation (Marble, TripoSplat).

ArtCraft is the flagship of a wider "Crafting Apps" line hosted in the same storytold GitHub org (70+ repos as of Oct 2026): PhotoCraft (photo editor), LightCraft (non-destructive photo editor), FilmCraft (nonlinear video editor), VectorCraft (vector art), PdfCraft (PDF reader/editor), FigCraft (design/prototyping), EffectCraft (motion graphics/compositing), SoundCraft (Pro Tools-style audio), CADCraft (AutoCAD-style CAD), WordCraft (word processor), and reported Microsoft Office clones (GridCraft/Excel, DeckCraft/PowerPoint). Each is billed as a clean-room reimplementation "written from scratch in pure Rust" of an established piece of professional creative or office software. Press coverage (Tom's Hardware, PetaPixel, and others) attributes most of this output to one developer, Brandon Thomas (Atlanta-based, ~15 years' experience, operating as the ArtCraft team/Storyteller.ai), who has stated he built the apps using Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 over a span of days in late September/early October 2026.

## Security

ArtCraft's own repository is dual-licensed MIT/Apache-2.0; licenses on the sibling Crafting Apps repos were not individually checked. The Crafting Apps repos drew unusually fast GitHub star growth — one analysis measured PhotoCraft going from roughly 16.7k to 62.9k stars within 8 days, and the full set of repos passing 100k combined stars within two weeks of release — which several outlets, including Tom's Hardware, treated as disputed rather than confirmed organic adoption. Tom's Hardware also identified Thomas as the author of related Reddit posts (in r/Adobe and r/creativecloud) that were subsequently removed by those communities. The codebases are reported as largely AI-generated by a single developer within days; "AI slop"/vibecoded-quality criticism followed the project's virality, which Thomas has publicly disputed. No independent security audit of any Crafting Apps codebase was found. Desktop builds are distributed as prebuilt Windows/macOS binaries in addition to source.
