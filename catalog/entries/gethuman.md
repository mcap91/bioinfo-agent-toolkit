## What it does

Crowdsourced database of customer service contact information:
- Direct phone numbers that skip automated menus
- Average wait times by time of day
- Step-by-step IVR navigation (e.g., "press 2, then 0, then say 'representative'")
- Callback scheduling service
- Coverage for thousands of companies

## Assessment

General utility — not AI/agent specific. Potential use case: data source for an agent that needs to contact support on behalf of a user, or reference for IVR automation tooling.
