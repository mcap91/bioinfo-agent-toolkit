## What it covers

A beginner-oriented Python book by Al Sweigart (Fellow of the Python Software Foundation; maintainer of PyAutoGUI and pyperclip), now in its 3rd edition, free to read online under a Creative Commons license (also sold in print/ebook). Early chapters teach fundamentals — Python basics, flow control, loops, functions, debugging, lists, dictionaries, strings, and regular expressions. Later chapters are project-driven automation of everyday tasks.

## Mechanical details

- **Automation topics covered:** reading/writing files, organizing files (copy/move/rename/compress), command-line programs, web scraping, Excel spreadsheets, Google Sheets, SQLite databases, PDF and Word documents, CSV/JSON/XML, scheduling and launching programs, sending email/texts/push notifications, graphs and image manipulation, OCR (recognizing text in images), controlling the keyboard and mouse, and text-to-speech / speech recognition.
- **Companion workbook** turns the reading into exercises and mini-projects; a video course follows much of the 1st-edition content (first 15 videos free on YouTube).
- 1st and 2nd editions remain available; supporting files downloadable from the site. Author's other beginner Python books are free at inventwithpython.com.

## Security

- Learning resource (book) — no executable component of its own beyond the example scripts the reader writes.
- **License:** free to read online under a Creative Commons license (Sweigart's books are typically CC BY-NC-SA); print/ebook sold commercially. Verify the exact CC variant before redistribution.
- Example projects use standard and third-party Python packages (e.g. PyAutoGUI for mouse/keyboard control) — normal Python supply-chain considerations apply when running the code.
