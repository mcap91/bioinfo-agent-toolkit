## Key claims

- Two versions of the same fireball effect differ only by applying classic animation principles, not by adding more particles or better shaders
- The principles originate from Disney animators in the 1930s
- Step one: anticipation — the hero leans back while the fire gathers, then throws
- Step two: squash and stretch — the fireball stretches in flight, and the enemy squashes on hit
- Step three: follow through — the hero's body settles after the throw, the trail lags behind, and the burst flies on
- Step four: timing — freeze the game for a few frames on the hit
- Step five: arcs — nothing flies straight, not the sparks, not the damage numbers
- These principles are 90 years old and still work
