## Key claims

- A card with no effects feels as unengaging as filling in a form
- Adding five effects (Hover, Pick Up, Sway, Snap, Sound) makes playing a card feel satisfying
- Hover: cards pop up and lean toward the cursor as you slide across the hand, but barely react when grabbed
- Pick Up: lifting a card spreads its shadow outward, though the card stays flat while dragged
- Sway: the card leans in the drag direction and swings back when released, but originally teleported on drop
- Snap: the card flies into its slot, overshoots, and settles, with the hand sliding to close the gap
- Sound: adding a soft flick sound on grab and a slap sound on landing makes every card feel like a big play
- Layering these five effects transforms the before (flat, lifeless) card into a satisfying after version
