## Caption

If your game has fire, poison and bleed, and the only difference is how hard they hit, players will pick the strongest one and the other two are dead weight.

The difference shouldn't be the number. It should be what each one does:

🔥 Fire spreads: when an enemy dies burning, it lights up the three closest ones.
☠️ Poison weakens: every ten stacks, the enemy takes 10% more damage from anything.
🩸 Bleed bursts: at five stacks, it deals all its remaining damage at once.

Now each one has a job: fire for hordes, poison for long fights and bleed for taking down one target, fast.

Capy of Gods: free demo on Steam, link in bio. Out November 9.
