[  29.3] up the next ones.
[  31.1] Poison softens them up.

[  39.0] 5 stacks, it bursts.
[  41.0] All the damage it had left at once.

[  46.8] Fire is for hordes, poison for long fights, and bleed for taking down one target fast.
