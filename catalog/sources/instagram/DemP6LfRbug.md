## Key claims

- Health bars don't jump instantly on damage; they drain smoothly instead
- A chunk is left behind briefly (white/orange) that blends down to red, so you see what you lost
- The bar shakes, flashes, and 'punches' on every hit
- Bar color (green/yellow/red) communicates how much trouble you're in
- Damage numbers pop up, float, and fade out
- Fast consecutive hits stack and arc to alternating sides
- Number size scales with hit size (small hit = small number, big hit = big number)
- Numbers are color-coded by type: white for normal, orange for crits, green for heals
- Critical hit numbers are bold and shake
- Healing fills the bar smoothly with sparkle effects at the edge
- These visual/UI techniques make the same underlying damage value feel different emotionally
