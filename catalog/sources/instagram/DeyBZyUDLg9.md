## Caption

Aleph: Download a Piece of the World

You can download a textured three dimensional model of a landmark, complete with high resolution satellite imagery and street view photos, without using a single API key. This tool is called Aleph, and it works by letting you draw a simple shape on a map to grab every physical detail inside that box. It pulls terrain data, building outlines, and Google Earth meshes directly to your laptop. The project was built specifically to give artificial intelligence models a direct connection to real world geography, allowing them to understand the physical layout of a place.
