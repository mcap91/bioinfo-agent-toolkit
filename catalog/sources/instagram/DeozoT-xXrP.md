## Key claims

- A boring boss entrance is just a bar popping up with no buildup
- Locking the door (path crumbling behind the player) removes escape and raises stakes
- Making the player wait with an empty arena, low rumble, and dust builds tension
- Revealing the boss slowly from feet to face increases epicness
- Slamming the boss's name on screen in big text adds impact
- A health bar filling up from empty shows how much health the boss has
- Low, heavy, slow drums in the music enhance the mood
- Keeping the camera low and shaking the ground with each step sells the boss's size
- A roar with screen shake, dust blast, and arena reaction completes the epic entrance
- Same boss can feel totally different depending on entrance design
